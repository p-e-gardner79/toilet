# Rhythm throughout the campaign

All four main levels teach and use SLOW → FAST → GET READY → PUSH AND HOLD → RELEASE. The plunger bonus keeps its separate ring-timing mechanic.

| Main level | Relief target | Warning before holding | Release window | Minimum continuous hold for release bonus |
|---|---:|---:|---:|---:|
| 1 · Banana Bandit | 110 | 2.20 s | 1.60 s | 0.70 s |
| 2 · The Final Rep | 125 | 2.00 s | 1.40 s | 1.00 s |
| 3 · Royal Blockage | 140 | 1.75 s | 1.20 s | 1.20 s |
| 4 · Sumo Showdown | 150 | 1.50 s | 1.00 s | 1.50 s |

Slow phases last four seconds; fast phases 2.5 seconds; held pushes 5.6 seconds to accommodate the full exaggerated voice performance. Fast-tap cues suggest roughly three taps per second initially and four at Level 4. The earliest accepted slow-tap interval tightens from 320ms to 440ms, making frantic input less effective. Fast taps are capped at minimum intervals of 200/180/160/145ms to prevent unrestricted input farming.

Successful slow taps give 2.6 relief, fast taps 1.6, held pressure up to 14 per cycle, and a timely release 12. A main round still lasts at most 35 seconds and clears at 100% for at least 3,000 points in that attempt. Existing scene events follow relief progress. Each character visibly strains while holding, and the recorded coach follows the phase changes from Level 1 onward.

See `METER-SOUND-PROMPTS.md` for the three requested sound assets. Their hooks respond only to successful effort, with pitch driven by meter progress. The game remains silent on these new meter channels until supplied recordings are added. Existing music, voice and scene sounds remain active.
